About the Founder

Aahana Chugh —

Rise and Shine was founded by Aahana Chugh, affectionately known to students and parents across Nagpur as Aahana Ma'am. Over the past two decades, she has become a familiar and deeply respected name among families in the city — known for her warm teaching style, engaging classes, and genuine passion for helping children grow in confidence and ability.

With more than 20 years of teaching experience, Aahana Ma'am has worked with hundreds of students across different age groups and academic boards. Her approach to education goes beyond textbooks — she believes that children learn best when they are curious, confident, and actively involved in the learning process.

Many parents in Nagpur trust Rise and Shine not only for academic support but also for helping children develop clarity in reading, pronunciation, writing, and expression — the foundations that serve them well beyond school.

What We Offer

Programs Designed for Every Stage
of Your Child's Journey

From the first sounds of phonics to the nuances of Cambridge literature — we have a thoughtfully crafted program for where your child is right now, and where they're headed.

🔤

Phonics Program

Early Learning
👶 Ages 4–8 | Pre-Primary to Grade 3

Phonics Program

A strong foundation in reading and pronunciation is the single greatest gift you can give an early learner. Our Phonics Program is designed for CBSE and Cambridge students who are just beginning their reading journey.

Through a structured, play-based approach, children learn to recognise letter sounds, blend them into words, and read with confidence and fluency.

  • Letter-sound recognition and blending techniques
  • Pronunciation and articulation practice
  • Sight words and early reading fluency
  • CBSE and Cambridge curriculum aligned
  • Small batch sizes for focused attention
📖

Language Development

English Skills
📚 Grades 3–10 | All Boards

Language Development

Strong English language skills open doors throughout your child's academic and professional life. This program focuses on building well-rounded English proficiency across all key areas.

  • Vocabulary building and contextual usage
  • Grammar — structure, rules, and application
  • Reading comprehension strategies
  • Creative and structured writing skills
  • Sentence construction and expression
🗣️

Spoken English & Communication

Confidence Building
🎤 Grades 4–12 | All Boards

Spoken English & Communication Skills

In today's world, how your child expresses themselves matters as much as what they know. This program builds the spoken confidence that allows children to thrive in school presentations, interviews, and everyday life.

  • Fluency and conversational English practice
  • Public speaking and presentation skills
  • Debate, discussion, and argumentation
  • Interview preparation and professional communication
  • Pronunciation, tone, and non-verbal communication
🎓

Cambridge & IB English Support

Cambridge & IB
🌍 Grades 1–12 | Cambridge IGCSE & IB

Cambridge & IB English Support

International curriculum students face a unique set of challenges in English — from nuanced comprehension questions to sophisticated essay writing. Aahana Ma'am's deep familiarity with Cambridge and IB requirements makes this program especially effective.

  • IGCSE English Language & Literature preparation
  • IB English A & B curriculum support
  • Advanced comprehension techniques
  • Essay, report, and analytical writing
  • Grammar for Cambridge and IB exam contexts
  • Past paper practice and exam strategy
📐

All-Subject Tuition Classes

All Subjects
📝 Grades 1–12 | CBSE, ICSE, State Board, IGCSE

All-Subject Tuition Classes

When your child needs comprehensive academic support, Rise and Shine's all-subject tuition program provides exactly that. Covering all major subjects across multiple boards, this program ensures your child is well-prepared and confident in every subject.

  • All core subjects for Grades 1–12
  • State Board, CBSE, ICSE, and IGCSE curricula
  • Regular assessments and progress tracking
  • Exam preparation and revision strategies
  • Homework support and concept clearing
  • Parent progress updates and communication
