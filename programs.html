Grammar Mastery

Good grammar is the backbone of clear communication. This focused program takes students from uncertainty to confident command of English grammar — with a practical, application-first approach that makes rules stick.

"Grammar is not about memorising rules. It's about internalising a way of thinking clearly."
  • Parts of speech, tenses, and sentence structure
  • Punctuation, prepositions, and determiners
  • Sentence transformation and error correction
  • Exam-pattern practice with full answer keys
  • Application in writing — not just isolated drills
