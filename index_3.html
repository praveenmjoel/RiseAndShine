Rise and Shine is more than a learning centre — it is a place where children discover who they are and what they are truly capable of.

Founded and led by Aahana Chugh, affectionately known as Aahana Ma'am, the institute has become one of Nagpur's most trusted names in education — known for its warm approach, exceptional results, and deep commitment to every child's growth.

The Founder

Aahana Chugh — a teacher who changed lives.

With more than 20 years of teaching experience, Aahana Ma'am has worked with hundreds of students across different age groups and academic boards. Her approach to education goes beyond textbooks — she believes children learn best when they are curious, confident, and actively involved.

Her classrooms are known for being lively, interactive, and thoughtfully structured to ensure that every child feels encouraged to participate and improve.

"Every child deserves a teacher who believes in them — not just in their marks, but in their voice, their thinking, and their potential."

At Rise and Shine, the focus is on building strong academic foundations while nurturing communication skills, critical thinking, and a love for learning that serves children well beyond school.

What we offer

Programs designed for every stage of your child's journey.

From the first sounds of phonics to the nuances of Cambridge literature — a thoughtfully crafted program for where your child is right now, and where they are headed.

PhonicsLanguage DevelopmentSpoken EnglishCambridge & IBAll-Subject Tuition

Phonics Program

Ages 4–8 · Pre-Primary to Grade 3 · CBSE & Cambridge

Phonics Program

A strong foundation in reading and pronunciation is the greatest gift you can give an early learner. Our structured, play-based phonics program ensures children build reading skills the right way — from the very beginning.

  • Letter-sound recognition and blending techniques
  • Pronunciation clarity and speech confidence
  • Sight words and early reading fluency
  • CBSE and Cambridge curriculum aligned
  • Small batch sizes for focused attention

Language Development

Grades 3–10 · All Boards

Language Development

Strong English language skills open doors throughout your child's academic and professional life. This program builds well-rounded proficiency across vocabulary, grammar, comprehension, and writing.

  • Rich vocabulary building with contextual usage
  • Grammar — rules, exceptions, and real application
  • Reading comprehension with advanced strategies
  • Creative and formal writing skills
  • Weekly assignments with individual written feedback

Spoken English & Communication

Grades 4–12 · All Boards

Spoken English & Communication Skills

How your child expresses themselves matters as much as what they know. This program builds the spoken confidence that allows children to thrive in presentations, interviews, and everyday life.

  • Fluency and conversational English
  • Public speaking and presentation skills
  • Debate, discussion, and argumentation
  • Pronunciation, tone, and body language
  • Interview preparation and professional communication

Cambridge & IB English Support

Grades 1–12 · Cambridge IGCSE & IB

Cambridge & IB English Support

International curriculum English demands sophisticated analytical thinking and nuanced writing. Aahana Ma'am's deep familiarity with Cambridge and IB frameworks makes this program especially effective.

  • IGCSE English Language & Literature preparation
  • IB English A & B curriculum support
  • Analytical and discursive essay writing
  • Poetry, prose, and drama comprehension
  • Past paper practice with detailed written feedback

All-Subject Tuition

Grades 1–12 · CBSE, ICSE, State Board, IGCSE

All-Subject Tuition Classes

When your child needs comprehensive academic support, Rise and Shine's all-subject tuition program covers all major subjects across multiple boards, with regular assessments and dedicated parent communication.

  • All core subjects across Grades 1–12
  • State Board, CBSE, ICSE, and IGCSE curricula
  • Regular assessments and progress tracking
  • Exam preparation and revision strategies
  • Dedicated parent-teacher communication
